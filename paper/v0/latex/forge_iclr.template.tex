% ---------------------------------------------------------------- typography
% The ICLR measure is narrower than the journal template's, so material that fits there
% overflows here. Rather than reflowing the source, the three offenders are sized to the
% column: verbatim pseudocode, wide supplementary tables, and chemical strings in cells.
% Headings carry their own numbers in the source, so that every cross-reference in the
% prose points at a number the author controls. Letting LaTeX number them as well produced
% "2 2 Reaction-Resolved Generation".
