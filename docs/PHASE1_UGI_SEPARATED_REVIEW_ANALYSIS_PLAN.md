## Fixed summaries

1. Identity: total, assessable, correct, incorrect and unassessable counts for all pairs and
   separately for redraws and diagnostic controls. Assessable means an explicit same/different
   judgment. Identity answers are compared with the authenticated complete canonical graphs.
2. Plausibility and legibility: independent A/B/tie/unassessable counts for the same three
   populations. Never infer a tie from the identity answer or reinterpret one field as the other.
3. Redraw cross-tabulation: all sixteen combinations of plausibility and legibility preferences,
   including zeros. Also report agreement and disagreement among pairs where both preferences
   are directional, with that denominator explicitly retained. Ties and abstentions remain in
   the full cross-tabulation and are not converted into directional choices.
4. Joint depiction style: for each preference field among redraws, count directional choices
   favoring the 0-degree/non-reversed style or the 117-degree/reversed style, plus ties and
   unassessable responses. The two rendering factors vary together. This is an association,
   without a causal decomposition or an inferential test.
5. Redraw concerns: count both-yes, both-no, opposing resolved flags, both-unassessable and
   one-side-unassessable. Report resolved-pair agreement with its denominator as well as all
   eight pairs. Missing responses are invalid captures; explicit unassessable flags are retained.
6. Diagnostic controls by origin: for baseline and measured-TRAIN slots separately, count
   preferred/other-preferred/tie/unassessable for both preference fields and yes/no/unassessable
   concerns, with presentation counts. Measured origin is not a plausibility ground truth.
7. Confidence: low/medium/high counts for all pairs and each pair kind. Do not weight or filter
   any outcome by confidence.
8. Joined rows: preserve every original response and verbatim reason, its original concern
   choices, pair kind, identity answer and outcome, and source/depiction metadata used for
   summaries. Keep this analytical output separate from the public blank review form.

## Interpretation boundaries

There are no p-values, bootstrap intervals, automatic pass/fail rule, new reviewer-calibration
threshold, score averaging or selective exclusions. The diagnostic is small, uses unique graphs
conditional on prior-packet exclusion, and changes instructions and presentation jointly. It
does not estimate population realism, isolate a causal presentation effect, establish baseline
superiority or compare a new treatment with its matched control. Before/after review differences
may be described later only as exploratory comparisons of different packets and instructions.

Biological-property comments remain unverified hypotheses. A concrete reported structural concern
may nominate an independent source-grounded audit. A model intervention still requires a justified
mechanism, an output-blind matched design and all existing realism, structural-validity, exact-L1,
diversity, novelty and fresh-confirmation requirements. No model is promoted by this analysis.

Development and tests use synthetic responses and synthetic keys only. The actual review remains
pending until the reviewer supplies the completed export. Record exact input and source hashes,
archive the executed source, preserve failed attempts, and write each analysis to a fresh directory.
