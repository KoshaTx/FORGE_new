## Finding

Mapping every LNPDB lipid is useful, provided the map distinguishes known evidence from chemical
possibility. The ledger now covers all 12,837 unique canonical ionizable lipids represented by the
19,797 raw LNPDB records. Each lipid links to its exact component identities, source families, zero or
more reusable route records, and separate route, procurement, and execution states.

The map does not claim to enumerate every chemically possible synthesis. An empty route list means
that no route has yet been curated for that component. It does not mean that the component is
unsynthesizable.

## AGILE route records

The hash-pinned AGILE measured file is the complete 20 by 12 by 5 Cartesian
library of 20 amines, 12 aldehyde-esters and 5 isocyanides, with 1,200 nominal
experimental records. After assay-structure reconciliation, 1,100 of those
records define unique single-compound graphs suitable for an oracle.

Supplementary Note 1 supports 24 structure-resolved component route records:

| Component class | Measured components | Additional source products | Structure-resolved routes | Route depth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aldehydes | 12 | 6 | 17 | 1 or 2 |
| Isocyanides | 5 | 2 | 7 | 2 |
| Amines | 20 | 0 | 0 | Not applicable to the reported L1 inputs |

The aldehyde routes include EDC-mediated esterification followed by Dess-Martin oxidation for
six-carbon and four-carbon spacers, plus direct Dess-Martin oxidation for olealdehyde and
palmitaldehyde. The isocyanide routes contain amine formylation followed by phosphorus oxychloride
dehydration. Every route record stores exact reactant, intermediate, and target structures, reported
conditions, source location, and evidence state. B5 is not among those 24 records.

The publication reports that the 20 amine headgroups and starting compounds were purchased from
Sigma-Aldrich and TCI America. This is retained as a historical vendor claim only. The paper does not
provide the exact catalog identifiers, purchase date, region-specific availability, or internal stock
records needed for procurement closure.

## Source reconciliation

The mapping preserves the raw LNPDB strings and uses the hash-pinned AGILE measured file as the
component-label reference for the `YX_2024` records.

Five labels in the raw LNPDB export omit or incompletely encode alkene stereochemistry relative to the
measured AGILE file: B4, B5, B11, B12, and C5. The C2 LNPDB component string encodes a
formamide-like structure, while the measured file and Supplementary Note 1 identify tetradecyl
isocyanide. The ledger does not rewrite these raw values. It records exact, stereo-reconciled, and
raw-conflict-preserved match bases separately.

B5 product identity and B5 route identity are now separated. The LANTERN audit
resolves the measured B5 product as the pure-trans compound and identifies B4
as a cis/trans mixture. The supplementary information still names an
undec-2-enoate, depicts 2-undecenoic acid and contains a chain-length conflict.
Therefore B5 is resolved for the single-structure oracle, but an exact upstream
B5 route remains unassigned.

After route-source reconciliation, 1,100 of the 1,200 nominal AGILE records
have source evidence for all three input components: a historical vendor claim
for A and extracted routes for B and C. The 100 products that contain B5 retain
an explicit upstream-route discrepancy, even though their product identity is
resolved for biological modeling.

## Generator integration

The product prior remains a whole-molecule discrete flow model. After a generated lipid receives one
or more candidate L1 disconnections, each exact component can retrieve zero or more source-linked L2
route directed acyclic graphs. A later hybrid route system can propose additional routes and rank
alternatives by evidence, applicability, depth, procurement, and uncertainty.

This representation supports multiple routes per component without asking the product generator to
assemble a lipid piecemeal. It also prevents a plausible route template from silently changing the
generated molecular identity.

## Scope and next evidence

This is an M0-09 inventory artifact. It does not implement or train the L2 model. The next route
curation tranche is the 26 PMC-linked LNPDB publication families, followed by acquisition of the 16
publisher or author packages and a time-stamped commercial catalog snapshot.

Before any learned route evaluation, route records must be split by publication and component family.
This prevents the same source series or near-identical component route from appearing in both training
and evaluation.
