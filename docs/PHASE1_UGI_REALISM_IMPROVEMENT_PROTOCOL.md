# Ugi realism improvement with preserved structural validity

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: active research; improvement is not yet demonstrated.

## Objective and scope

Demonstrate improved lipid realism while maintaining current structural validity, exact Ugi
reconstruction, diversity, and novelty, without weakening any frozen gate. Sampling attribution
is a prerequisite for choosing an intervention, not the completion criterion for this goal.

Use the authorized computational Phase 1 product/L1 system. Preserve the checkpoint, training
partitions, source evidence, registry, constitutional identity convention, atom/size support, and
all historical negative results. The initial investigation uses local CPU inference only and no
biological guidance, routing, procurement, prospective selection, repair, or retry. This protocol
does not allocate paid compute or authorize retry of a failed paid job.

## Baseline and competing explanations

The comparator is the frozen amine-semantic joint-support decoder at checkpoint step 9143 of
`shared_bias_parallel_program_role_seed0_v2`. It is a comparison baseline; its existence is not
evidence of production promotion. The diagnostic workbench's treatment is the preceding
`ugi_donor_slack_tiered_head_group_entropy_terminal_offset_bondweight2` arm, with binary topology
support. Do not substitute the later topology-tier arm, which has different products and no
separate realism panel or visual assessment.

The saved comparison baseline produced 256/256 valid, verified exact-L1 products, 170 distinct
exact-L1 products, effective component count 31.399251893, and 236/256 products containing a novel
component. These values are recoverable from the hash-pinned comparison used by
`configs/multireaction/ugi_realism_evaluator_audit_v1.json`; they are historical observations, not
new measurements. The evaluated treatment's lower novelty prevents calling its descriptor gains
a solution to the present objective.

Competing explanations to distinguish before choosing a change:

1. The checkpoint already scores plausible legal arrangements poorly.
2. Legal support removes arrangements favored by the checkpoint.
3. Rank normalization, semantic scores, or entropy mixing substantially change neural preferences.
4. Some choices bypass the neural score, or a fixed neural prediction becomes inappropriate after
   another structural choice.
5. The apparent gain arises from component familiarity or evaluator resolution, without better
   novel structures.

## Passive sampling attribution

Authenticate the original program draw, checkpoint, decoder configuration, implementation, and
saved samples. Preserve the original sample steps, batch size, program order, and random streams.
The initial replay uses the complete first batch of 128 programs and records full decision details
for the first 16 prespecified attempts. Reducing a 128-row batch to 16 rows is not a historical
prefix replay because it changes batched random-number consumption.

Compare an endpoint-only observed control with a full decision trace at identical settings. Require equality of
every product row, topology, abstention, terminal state, complete sampler summary, and captured
RNG state. Separately compare the replayed rows with the corresponding saved historical rows.
Any mismatch is an operational finding to diagnose before using the trace for attribution.

Record the existing model predictions, ordered legal candidates, scores, actual selection law,
selected candidate, and accepted state. Include the tail-count frequency draw, which can bypass
the rank policy, and the completed-head entropy guard's final distribution. Capture actual
choices without new model evaluations, random draws, structural repairs, or candidate filtering.
Report restricted support and deterministic choices as such; do not invent an unconstrained
whole-graph probability law from coordinate logits.

Use bounded sidecar observation with explicit overflow failures. Restore observational bindings
on success and failure. Record timing and input/source hashes. Output equality, not the observer's
intent, establishes that tracing was passive. Shared arm seed labels alone do not establish
per-attempt random-choice coupling when the arms consume different numbers of random draws.

The sampler's `exact_tensor` and `exact_target_graph` compare against layout placeholders. They
must never be used as exact-L1 metrics. Compute exact L1 with the existing constitutional reverse
decomposition and exact forward verifier. Read training identities only for novelty assessment;
do not invoke the broad R0 heldout assessor while choosing an intervention. The existing cache
loader reads packed arrays containing all folds; only TRAIN records are materialized for layouts.
Heldout structures must not be interpreted, decomposed, or fingerprinted.

## Additive preservation requirements

Retain every existing structural, quantitative, and visual gate. Add the following observed
no-loss requirements for the user's objective before examining successor outputs:

- Validity and exact-L1 yield must each be at least the paired baseline's observed level. For
  the saved 256-request comparison that level is 100%, stronger than the historical 95% floor.
- No program-support abstentions, fixed-state violations, repairs, retries, or filtered attempts.
- Effective component count, unique exact-L1 yield, mean pairwise fingerprint distance, products
  containing a novel component, whole-product novelty, and unique novel exact-L1 yield must each
  be at least the paired baseline's value. Report numerator and denominator for each proportion.
- Report novel-component incidence and distinct/effective count separately for every precursor
  role. An aggregate gain cannot justify an undisclosed role-level loss.

These are additional requirements, not replacements for frozen thresholds. Passing a small sample
does not prove population equivalence; the final evidence must retain uncertainty and its scope.

## Realism evidence and progression

Use the unchanged measured-Ugi train-only role panel: attempt-weighted and unique-product-weighted
Wasserstein, energy distance, and MMD-squared must all improve. Retain failed/invalid/duplicate and
out-of-support attempts in the prescribed denominators. The admitted-reference subset never
replaces the full frozen reference.

Also report complementary per-role graph distances with familiarity and novelty incidence. The
both-novel strata are post-treatment descriptive comparisons, not causal estimates. Fingerprint
proximity and descriptor improvements alone cannot satisfy the realism objective.

Retain the frozen blinded appearance criteria, including both head–tail balance and tail morphology,
assessable-pair minima, material preference margin, and pathology limits. Calibrate review using
identical-graph redraws and measured controls, preserve reviewer identity, and do not present an
automated judge as an independent human reviewer. Reviewer outcomes must be recorded from actual
review; pending calibration is not a pass.

After attribution, freeze one mechanism-specific successor and its exact matched comparison before
generation. Use the frozen baseline and nearest failed treatment as controls. Preserve all failed
interventions. Do not repeat scalar-weight sweeps or previously failed training changes unchanged.
Stop an arm at a violated invariant or preservation requirement, retaining the complete failure
receipt and all attempted outputs.

A development pass permits preparing a separate confirmation protocol with fresh, output-blind
requests and review rows. Reused seed-0 development outputs cannot be confirmation. Define the
confirmation budget, sampling units, uncertainty, and decision before inspecting its outputs.
Keep checkpoint-specific improvements distinct from claims across independently trained models.
