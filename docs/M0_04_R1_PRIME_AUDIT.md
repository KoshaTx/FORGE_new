# M0-04: Cross-platform Reaction-registry Coverage and Leakage Audit

The authoritative constitutional-graph audit is generated by `make m0-04`.
Machine-readable results and per-structure distances are in
`results/m0_04_constitutional/`. The earlier isomeric-string result and its
per-platform addendum remain frozen in `results/m0_04/` as historical controls.

## Finding

Train-derived reaction enumeration improves exact non-AGILE recovery under
headgroup and linker/scaffold holdouts. R1-prime recovers 205/2,065 non-AGILE
headgroup-held-out lipids (9.93%, 95% Wilson interval 8.71% to 11.29%) and
341/2,224 non-AGILE linker/scaffold-held-out lipids (15.33%, 13.90% to
16.89%). Across all held-out structures, including AGILE, recovery is
260/2,285 (11.38%) and 402/2,285 (17.59%), respectively. It recovers
0/2,328 source-study-held-out lipids (0%, upper 95% Wilson bound 0.165%).

The corrected component-family stress test contains 1,100 single-compound
AGILE structures and no non-AGILE structures. R1-prime recovers 100/1,100
exactly (9.09%, 7.53% to 10.94%). The 100 B4 mixture measurements are excluded
from single-graph supervision. This split remains a secondary stress test, as
established in M0-03.

The result is split-dependent. R1-prime is better anchored than the existing
corpus for two non-AGILE component-based holdouts. The 0/2,328 source-study result is a cross-platform leakage and
reaction-registry coverage result, not a model-generalization score. It shows that training-study
components passed through the 12 frozen transforms did not recreate an exact held-out product. It
does not show that the whole-lipid generator cannot learn held-out lipid structures, that the held-out
lipids are not synthesizable, or that a future family-specific adapter could not route them. R1-prime
remains a diagnostic reaction-enumerated reference set, not the molecular generator and not a
route-certified corpus.

## What the source-study holdout measures

Three distinct forms of coverage must not be conflated:

1. **Motif coverage:** whether training structures contain related hydrophobic chains, branching,
   unsaturation, ester placement, or other local motifs.
2. **Role-compatible component coverage:** whether the exact required reactants exist in the roles
   declared by a frozen transformation. For Ugi 3-CR, a hydrophobic motif must be realized in an
   aldehyde or isocyanide component, not merely occur somewhere in another lipid.
3. **Exact product coverage:** whether frozen transformations applied to training-derived components
   reconstruct the identical held-out molecular graph.

Cross-platform motif coverage can be substantial while exact product coverage is zero. A shared C18
or cis-unsaturated hydrophobe does not preserve the platform-specific attachment atoms, central core,
or component roles. This distinction is a reason to keep the whole-lipid generator's support separate
from the reaction enumerator's support.

## Methods

The audit loaded the frozen M0-03 assignments and the 12 qualified atom-mapped transforms from the
hash-pinned vendored registries. For each training structure, each reverse outcome was sanitized,
required to be a single molecule per declared role, checked against the role's required handle,
allowed site multiplicity, forbidden SMARTS, and registry selectivity contract, and then required to
reconstruct the original canonical isomeric structure through the forward transform. Every registry
positive and negative example passed before corpus processing began.

Accepted blocks retain reaction role, occurrence count, source-structure count, and source-study
provenance. Cartesian products were enumerated separately within each reaction family. Products were
sanitized, canonicalized, and exactly deduplicated in a disk-backed SQLite index. Existing R1 and
R1-prime recovery were then evaluated only on the frozen held-out fold.

The authoritative schema-v3 run used Python 3.14.2, RDKit 2025.09.6, SQLite
3.51.2, four scheme processes, deterministic input ordering, and no randomness. It applies the source-qualified
symmetry-distinct-site policy for the Ugi amine role. Enumeration was exhaustive and retained the
declared molecular support.

The source-platform addendum re-ran reverse and exact forward reconstruction on the 2,333 held-out
products only for diagnosis. It did not harvest any held-out component. Each exact alternative
decomposition was joined by reaction ID, role, and canonical component structure to the frozen
source-study training pool. The complete per-product outcome is
`source_platform_registry_coverage.csv.gz`; its hash and all input hashes are recorded in
`source_platform_registry_audit.json`.

## Decision and limitations

1. Keep R0 real lipids as the structural anchor.
2. Retain the existing large reaction-enumerated corpus only as weighted auxiliary structural data,
   using its declared realism weights rather than raw reaction-family counts.
3. Keep R1-prime diagnostic until M0-05 reports decomposition precision. Do not treat its millions of
   products as route labels or as the target molecular distribution.
4. Keep the primary product generator as a whole-graph discrete flow. Do not constrain generation to
   the audited block cross-product.
5. Evaluate broad source-held-out platforms structurally at the whole-lipid-model level. Evaluate
   synthesis generalization within Ugi chemistry using held heads, aldehyde and isocyanide classes,
   component pairs, structural clusters, and prospective E2 candidates.
6. Mine non-Ugi libraries for transferable hydrophobic motifs only through an explicitly versioned
   M0-09 transfer registry. Do not treat motif occurrence as an exact Ugi component, route label, or
   inherited biological outcome.
7. Use AGILE labels for a calibrated, applicability-gated soft oracle tilt. Shared Ugi chemistry
   supports this role, but component-family shifts show why uncertainty and M0-07 calibration remain
   required.
8. Use the M0-09 hybrid L2 design: generic neural proposal plus deterministic lipid-specific template
   search. R1 and R1-prime do not contain observed upstream reaction outcomes and cannot supervise L2.

M0-04 measures coverage, not chemical precision. The reverse decompositions in
`decomposition_candidates.csv.gz` are diagnostic candidates. M0-05's
source-grounded adjudication, rather than this enumeration or an unrecorded
human judgment, is the independent authority for admitting exact Ugi
supervision.
